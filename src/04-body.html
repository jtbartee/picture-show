    Every film here is streamed directly from the Internet Archive. Picture Show is only a lobby — it stores no video and hosts nothing. All titles come from the Feature Films, Sci-Fi / Horror, Saturday Morning Cartoons and Classic TV: 1980s collections at archive.org, a non-profit library. Please consider donating to them — they pay for the bandwidth this page uses.

    Films are listed here because the Archive distributes them as public domain or under an open licence. Rights vary by country and the Archive's item page is the authority — every title links back to it. Posters and metadata are the Archive's. Genre groupings are guessed from Archive metadata by hand-written rules, so expect the odd mis-filing.
